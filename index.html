Journey to Freedom

🕊️
1863

Emancipation Proclamation

President Lincoln declares enslaved people in Confederate states free.

Did you know? The proclamation did not immediately free all enslaved people!
🎉
June 19, 1865

Freedom in Texas

Union troops arrive in Galveston, TX, announcing freedom for all enslaved people.

Did you know? This is why Juneteenth is celebrated on June 19th!
🇺🇸
2021

Federal Holiday

Juneteenth becomes a U.S. federal holiday, recognized nationwide.

Did you know? Juneteenth is the first new federal holiday since 1983!
